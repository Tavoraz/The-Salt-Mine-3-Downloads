Try the dive yourself

  1. Find The Salt Mine 3 Playtest in your Steam Library and install it on Windows.
  2. Pick Moro, choose Quick battleground and select War for the Vein. Join the queue and accept the ready check. Select Lock in if prompted.
  3. Try Night Predator to take flight. Look for Cling & Dive on Moro’s hotbar to dive from above.

AI heroes fill open battleground seats. To queue with friends, group up with the battlemaster in Saltwake.
