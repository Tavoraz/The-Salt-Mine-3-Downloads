Whenever you’re ready

Prefer a solo round? Pick a hero, select Quick battleground, then choose Push the Cart. Join and accept the ready check. When the lobby opens, select Lock in. AI heroes fill empty slots.

Exploring solo? At the lantern arch, select Enter the Vault.

Meet by Battlemaster Rook in Saltwake. Choose Enter World. Walk through the SALTWAKE arch and look right for the wooden BATTLEGROUNDS board. Press F near Rook to open it. Looking for company? Press Enter to ask in town chat.

Decide who stays by the cart before you queue. One player escorts the cart while the other keeps defenders away. Anyone can take either job, and you can swap during the match. Compare your calls before you queue.

  1. Form a group. One person selects Form a group. The others select Join by that player’s name. Check that everyone appears on the board.
  2. Queue together. The leader chooses the mode above and selects Join battleground. Everyone selects Ready for battle. The ready check shows Waiting or Ready next to each name.
  3. Confirm your hero. Stay through the ready check. When the match lobby opens, each player selects Lock in for their hero. See both on-screen confirmations.

Meet at the lantern arch in Saltwake. Choose Enter World and head to the arch in the northeast garden.

  1. Form a group. One person selects Form a group at the arch. Friends select Join beside the leader’s name.
  2. Enter together. Everyone selects I’m ready. The leader then selects Enter together.

What if a teammate goes down? Make the rescue call with a friend.

Meet at the SALTWAKE VALE arch. Pick your hero and choose Enter World. Walk north around Saltwake’s central crystal beacon and follow the road under the arch. You can head out alone while your friend installs.

On the Vale side, press F to talk to Nell or Rowan, the Vale Warden. Each player should accept their quest before heading to Lowfield Orchard.

  • Something for the Road. Gather three windfall apples under the orchard trees. Return to Nell for 140 Adventure XP and Nell’s Traveller’s Knot, a health relic.
  • A Winter Stolen. Defeat three Wickpin raiders near the orchard. Return to Rowan for 230 Adventure XP and the Orchard Watchbadge, a power relic.

Either quest works solo. The default keys are M for your map, L for your quest journal and B for your bag.
